Please Read

Important Information for All Sessions

  • This is a non-competitive event, so there will be no scorekeeping or results.
  • Managers/coaches will coordinate their teams on their allocated pitch. League Coordinators will also be available to assist throughout the day.
  • Teams will rotate between games and play different opposition. This is designed to give children the opportunity to play against different players, teams and levels of challenge.
  • If teams have different squad sizes — for example one team has 6 players and another has 9 or 12 — managers should agree on the day how best to ensure all children get plenty of football. This could include formats such as 2v1 or 3v3 for additional players.
  • Please ensure coaches help FC Hanley keep the event running to time and move teams onto the next fixture promptly.
  • All parents must remain outside the playing surface.
  • Please ensure children have everything they need and are ready to play at their allocated times.

Thanks everyone for your support and cooperation in helping make the Welcome to Football events a great experience for all the children.
